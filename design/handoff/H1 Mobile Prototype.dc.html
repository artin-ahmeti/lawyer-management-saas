Component gaps
  • Waveform — voice-memo recording level. Props: levels[], live. Composed here from bars in --accent. Needed by Capture · Voice memo.
  • MonthGrid — 7-column month with event dots. Props: month, days[{date, dots[]}], selected. Composed from divs + Dot. Needed by Calendar · Month.
  • ReceiptSlot — dashed drop/camera target for a receipt photo. Props: image?, onCapture. Needed by Capture · Expense.
  • PlanSchedule — instalment preview (date · amount · state). Composed from List. Needed by Billing · Offer a payment plan.
  • OfflineBanner — Banner compact tone="info" icon="wifi-off" works, but a persistent variant that sits under the header without a margin would avoid per-screen glue.
Decisions log
  • Matter detail shows two mini KPIs (Unbilled, Trust), not three as in the reference screen; the design system caps phone screens at two tiles. Hours moved to the Unbilled delta.
  • Review your day is a grouped List under a section header (as in the reference), not a Card; Bill / Skip live in the row trail.
  • Payment plans show as one invoice with a Pill tone="warning" “Partial” and “plan 2 of 4” in the subtitle; the schedule appears only in the plan sheet.
  • Text pay link is a Dialog (one irreversible send), Record payment and Offer a plan are Sheets (they collect input).
  • Payment received is a state of Invoice detail (success banner + Paid pill + updated KPIs), not a separate screen; the client-side push is simulated from the side panel.
  • Courthouse mode adds a “Quick note” variant of the Note sheet, pre-pointed at the matter with a hearing today (People v. Webb, Dept. 22).
  • Contacts is reached from a header action on Matters in H1; it earns no tab.
  • Durations round up to 0.1h at save (12 min → 0.2h); the stepper moves in 6-minute steps.
