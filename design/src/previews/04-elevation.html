Content layers (lists, cards, tiles) are separated by line-hairline and a change of surface, never by shadow. Shadows mark the three things that float above the page: popovers, the running-timer bar and sheets/dialogs. In dark mode a 1px line-hairline ring replaces most of the shadow so edges stay visible.
