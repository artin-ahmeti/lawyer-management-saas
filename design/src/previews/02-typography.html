Rules
  • One family for everything you read. Geist at 400/500/600 from 11px to 32px; titles tighten to −0.02em so large text stays crisp. No display face: the hierarchy is size, weight and space.
  • Numbers are always tabular. Amounts, hours, dates in columns, timers, KPI tiles. Cents render in ink-secondary so the dollars carry.
  • Identifiers are mono. Matter and invoice numbers, UTBMS codes, account suffixes. It says “this is a record”, not prose.
  • Weight before colour. Hierarchy comes from size and 400/500/600 weight; colour is reserved for state and action.
  • Uppercase only at 11px overline, with +0.06em tracking. Pills and labels stay sentence case.
  • Line length 45–70 characters on web; mobile body wraps at the 20px gutter.
