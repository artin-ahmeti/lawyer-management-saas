A practice you can run from the courthouse hallway.

Clepso is the design system for the staff app (iOS and Android), the web app that follows, and the client app after that. It replaces the current dark navy-and-amber screens with a light-first, list-first system in cobalt and graphite, built around the four things the feature strategy says decide a small firm’s revenue: capturing time, getting paid, answering leads and keeping clients informed. Use the theme switch above to see every component and screen in light and in courthouse mode.
