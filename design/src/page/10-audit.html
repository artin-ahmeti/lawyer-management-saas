01 · Audit

Why the current system doesn’t hold up

The current app has one dark palette (navy canvas, amber accent, cream text), a four-step type scale on the system font, and nine primitives. Every screen is a stack of rounded cards on a dark background. It was a reasonable Phase-0 scaffold; it cannot carry a billing product that lawyers read in daylight, in court, and later on a 27-inch monitor. The findings below are ordered by how much they hurt.

1

No typographic system

The system font with four sizes (28 / 20 / 15 / 12) and no label, overline, amount or identifier styles. Numbers aren’t tabular, so amounts and hours wobble in lists; the timer uses a generic font-mono. Hierarchy comes from size alone, which is why every screen reads flat.

2

Card soup instead of lists

Matters, invoices, contacts and home sections are all floating rounded-lg bg-surface cards with 12px gaps and an 18px radius. A matter row costs ~90px, separators aren’t inset, and nothing distinguishes a list from a card from a tile. This is the “layout” problem: there is only one container, used for everything.

3

Dark-only, with hard-coded colours

The tokens file says “these ARE the dark theme values”; there is no light theme and the status bar is pinned light. Five hex literals (#FFB162, #1B2632, #8B94A3, #C9C1B1, #C96A50) are pasted into Button, Input, EmptyState, the tab layout and four screens, bypassing tokens entirely. Dense billing data on a phone in daylight needs a light theme; dark is the courthouse case, not the default.

4

The accent means everything, so it means nothing

Amber is the primary button, the warning colour, event icons, badges, unbilled amounts, filter chips, the active tab and the emphasis tile. Priority and overdue both use rust. Success is a muted olive that barely clears the surface. State and action share one hue, so nothing stands out. ink-faint on canvas is ~3.6:1, below the text minimum.

5

Two sources of truth, both incomplete

tokens.ts and tailwind-preset.cjs duplicate values with a “keep in sync” comment. Neither holds spacing, type, motion, elevation or sizes; neither has a light/dark structure; nothing is consumable by the web app without re-declaring it.

6

Navigation doesn’t match the strategy

Five equal tabs (Home, Matters, Tasks, Time, Billing). Capture is a whole tab with a timer and chips, not a one-tap action. Contacts hide behind the avatar; there is no calendar, no inbox, no lead surface. “Capture, don’t enter” needs capture reachable from every screen.

7

Nine primitives, no product components

Avatar, Badge, Button, Card, EmptyState, Input, Screen, SectionHeader, StatCard. No list row, segmented control, sheet, banner, toast, stage tracker, table, timer bar or form group. Badge is uppercase with wide tracking (shouty at 12px); Button has no icon slot or web sizes; Input has no affixes or states.

8

Details that add up

A full-amber “emphasis” tile competes with the primary button; section headers use the screen-title size; the tab bar is a fixed 84px with no safe-area logic; no focus states; no reduced-motion handling; demo credentials are printed on the sign-in screen.

What to keep
  • The 20pt screen gutter and the integer-minutes / integer-cents discipline in @lawfirm/core.
  • Dark mode as a real need. It becomes the second theme (“courthouse mode”), not the only one.
  • The stack: NativeWind, FlashList, the bottom-sheet library and the feature-slice structure all fit the new system without churn.
  • The idea of a running timer as a first-class object. It just needs to live on every screen instead of inside one tab.
