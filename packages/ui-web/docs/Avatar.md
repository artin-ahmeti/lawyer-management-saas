Initials on a tonal surface; people are round, organisations are squared.

Sizes `xs` 24 · `sm` 28 · `md` 36 · `lg` 48 · `xl` 64. `tone="accent"` marks the signed-in user; `tone="ink"` marks a "+3" count in an `AvatarStack`. No random colours.

```tsx
<Avatar name="Margaret Bennett" /> <Avatar kind="org" initials="KH" /> <Avatar kind="org" icon="building" />
```
