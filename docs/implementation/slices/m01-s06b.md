## October 8, 2026 — M01-S06b audited membership removal and restoration

**Locally complete slice; M01/M15 remain partial.** Planned and approved October 8,
2026 (commit `a6f95b9`), implemented in `a15e726`, `10b4b2f`, `0dcef0c` and `139dd08`
on `impl/core`. Blueprint p12/p26/p39/p40 were read from the PDF for this slice.
Before it, a removed membership could only be produced outside the product, its
matter grants stayed active, and nothing could restore it. Settings now lets
current owners/admins remove staff with a reason, list removed staff, restore a
membership with an explicitly reviewed role, inspect durable history and recover
an uncertain request with the same intent.

Prerequisites: M00 receipts/audit, M01 live account/session/firm context (D016),
S05b explicit matter grants (D018) and S06a role changes with firm-first policy
locks (D019). D020 records this slice's boundaries and accepted trade-offs.

Behaviour:

- `POST /firms/current/staff/removals` `{userId, expectedRevision, reason}` and
  `POST /firms/current/staff/restorations` `{userId, role, expectedRevision, reason}`
  with a required UUID `Idempotency-Key`; `GET /firms/current/staff/removed` and
  `/membership-history`, 20 rows with stable cursors. New capability
  `firm.staff.memberships.manage` for owner/admin, evaluated from the live role.
- Removal soft-deletes the membership, increments its revision, revokes every
  active matter grant (grant revision +1, matter `access_revision` +1, one matter
  history row with fixed text) and revokes the member's pending invitations, all
  with the receipt and audit in one transaction under the exclusive firm lock.
- Refusals: owner targets (removal, or restoring a former owner) and owner roles
  need a current owner; the last available owner cannot be removed; removing the
  last eligible manager of a live matter returns `MATTER_HANDOFF_REQUIRED` with no
  matter name, ID or count. An unavailable sole manager can be removed, since that
  lowers no eligible-manager count and stops their grant returning after a ban.
- Restoration requires an available account and an explicitly chosen role, and
  leaves zero active grants, including grants left by a membership removed
  outside this command. Matter managers grant access again where needed.
- Replays return the original acknowledgement only while the membership still
  matches it; changed input, stale review, no-op and changed state return
  distinct 409 codes. Self-removal commits once; its replay is denied (D018).
