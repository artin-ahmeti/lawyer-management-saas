## October 8, 2026 — M02-S04 jurisdiction, venue and governing-law references

**Slice locally complete; M02/M15 remain partial.** Planned and approved in this session
(the user approved the D023 boundary as proposed). Before this slice a matter had no
jurisdiction at all: no governing law, venue, agency or docket, and nothing distinguished
the firm's address or lawyers' admissions from where a matter sits. Staff can now record
none, one or several references per matter from a fixed US catalog, optionally naming a
firm forum and a docket number, and every reference states that no jurisdiction-specific
automation applies.

Prerequisites: M01 live firm/session context and firm-first policy locks
(D016/D019/D020), durable matters with explicit grants (D017/D018), contacts and parties
(D021), profiles and pinned fields (D022). Blueprint pages read for this slice: p13 (M02
step 4 and data additions), p34–36 (practice and jurisdiction coverage), p39
(nationwide-model acceptance).
