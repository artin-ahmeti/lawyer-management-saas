## October 8, 2026 — M02-S02 universal contacts and matter-party links

**Slice locally complete; M02/M15 remain partial.** Planned and approved in this
session (the user chose the recommended D021 boundary). Before this slice the
Contacts screen and every party were fixtures, and no contact or party table
existed. Live staff can now keep a firm directory of people and organizations,
find them by name, and link one contact to several matters or several contacts to
one matter as client, adverse party or a labelled other party. That meets the p13
scenario "represent one client in several matters and several clients in one
matter" on durable records with no court, docket or practice-area field. A contact
never reveals a matter the reader is not granted, its title or a count.

Prerequisites: M00 receipt/audit foundation; M01 live firm/session context (D016),
durable matters with explicit grants (D017/D018) and firm-first policy locks
(D019/D020). Blueprint pages read for this slice: p13 (M02), p29 (M18), pp34–38
(practice/jurisdiction coverage and flagship journeys).
